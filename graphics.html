Web Image Formats Breakdown

PNG: Lossless format optimal for transparent backgrounds, line art, and diagrams.
JPEG: Lossy compression best suited for complex photographic images with rich color gradients.
GIF: Low-color format designed for simple animated graphics.
SVG: Vector format that resizes smoothly across displays without loss of quality.
