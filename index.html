02 / ABOUT ME

The pursuit of depth over noise.

I study Computer Science in the Honors College at Georgia State University, concentrating on the mathematical mechanics that make software systems resilient and models trustworthy.

In an era of disposable templates and superficial wrappers, genuine engineering leverage belongs to those who understand the primitives: how statistical distributions behave under stress, how high-velocity telemetry moves through time-series databases, and how deterministic validation keeps autonomous agents honest.

My work is grounded in first principles. Whether computing a rolling logarithmic regression across volatile markets or routing physical IoT sensor data to protect data center hardware, I care about building systems that are mathematically defensible and built to last.

I measure progress not by how many tools I can skim, but by how deeply I understand what I construct from scratch.
