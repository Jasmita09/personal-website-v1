01 / about me

Listening for signal where others only see noise.

I'm a Computer Science student in the Honors College at Georgia State University, focusing on data science. I like taking messy, real-world data, such as sensor readings, market prices and text, and finding the patterns that actually hold up.

Rather than skimming surface-level AI tools, I care about understanding why a model works: how to check it, test it and trust it. Then I turn it into something people can use.

I'm honest about where I am: I know the machine learning basics and I'm building depth with every project. That's why I document my architecture and write about what I learn.
