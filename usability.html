User Navigation Task Evaluation

  1. Study Protocol:
    • Target System: Amazon E-Commerce Platform
    • Target User Group: General internet users
    • Evaluated Navigation Tasks:
      • Search for a specialized or rare product online.
      • Initiate order checkout and complete a cancellation.
      • Discover customer service support options.

  2. Study Results: Participants averaged 10 minutes and 8 distinct navigation clicks due to deep sub-menu structures.

  3. Design Recommendations: Integrating a persistent floating customer support button would streamline user assistance and reduce workflow drop-off.
